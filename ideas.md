# Lumora Dental Studio — Design Direction

## Three stylistic approaches

### Theme Name: Quiet Confidence
A warm, editorial healthcare identity with soft ivory surfaces, deep ink typography, and mineral teal accents. It should feel established and human, like a trusted practice with a calm point of view.

**Probability:** 0.03

### Theme Name: Coastal Clarity
A brighter, more clinical direction built from airy whites, pale aqua, and crisp navy with luminous photography and modular service panels. It communicates cleanliness and efficiency with a family-friendly tone.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Botanical Smile
A refined organic direction pairing muted sage, cream, and warm clay with tactile photography and gentle rounded forms. It makes preventive care feel restorative and lifestyle-oriented.

**Probability:** 0.02

## Chosen approach: Quiet Confidence

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial minimalism with influences from boutique hospitality and modern Swiss healthcare design.

### Core Principles
1. Use generous negative space and an asymmetric editorial rhythm rather than a generic centered marketing grid.
2. Pair a distinctive high-contrast display serif with a warm, highly legible sans-serif for confidence and approachability.
3. Use tactile surfaces—soft ivory, paper-like grain, hairline rules, and mineral teal—so the interface feels considered rather than sterile.
4. Make every interaction feel reassuring and purposeful: clear next steps, calm transitions, and no noisy conversion pressure.

### Color Philosophy
The palette is anchored in warm ivory rather than stark white to create a sense of ease. Deep ink navy provides authority and legibility; mineral teal is the ownable signature accent, used for moments of action and reassurance. A restrained clay tone adds humanity without becoming playful or cosmetic.

### Layout Paradigm
A left-anchored editorial composition: section labels sit in a narrow rail, while content unfolds in generous horizontal bands, offset cards, and occasional split-screen moments. The hero uses an intentional overlap between copy and imagery to create a magazine-like first impression.

### Signature Elements
- A small teal “plus” mark that appears beside section labels and becomes the brand icon.
- Hairline ivory/navy rules and small uppercase metadata for an editorial clinical vocabulary.
- Softly cropped photography framed like an art-directed contact sheet, with rounded corners used sparingly.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should reduce uncertainty. CTAs have a subtle lift and directional arrow, cards reveal a little more context on hover, and forms use clear helper copy and a calm success state. Focus rings are visible and intentional.

### Animation
Use short, smooth ease-out transitions for nav, buttons, accordions, and cards. Page sections reveal with a slight upward drift and opacity change, staggered by 50ms. Never animate critical form feedback or navigation in a way that slows the user. Respect prefers-reduced-motion by disabling entrance transforms.

### Typography System
Display: Cormorant Garamond, 600–700, used for hero and section headlines with compact line-height. Body/UI: Manrope, 400–700, used for navigation, paragraphs, labels, and form controls. Eyebrow labels are Manrope 700, uppercase, 0.14em tracking. Never use Inter.

### Brand Essence
Lumora is the calm, modern dental studio for people who want expert care without the clinical distance—through thoughtful dentistry, honest guidance, and a gentler experience.

**Personality:** reassuring, exacting, luminous.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are concise, confident, and human. CTAs are direct but never urgent or pushy. Microcopy explains what happens next and removes friction.

Example lines: “A healthier smile, thoughtfully cared for.” “Tell us what you need; we’ll take it from there.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The mark is a bold teal four-point plus with a softened inner counter, paired with a custom-feeling Lumora wordmark in a high-contrast serif. The plus mark is used independently as the favicon and recurring section signature.

### Signature Brand Color
**Mineral Teal — #2B7A78**, a grounded blue-green that feels clinical enough for trust but warm enough for human care.

## Style Decisions
- The homepage is the primary experience, with in-page anchors and a dedicated appointment route.
- Testimonials and before/after examples use clearly labeled illustrative placeholders or practice-provided content areas; no fabricated patient reviews, ratings, or outcomes are presented as real.
- Clinic details remain visibly replaceable: [Clinic Address], [Phone Number], [Email Address], and [Opening Hours].

## Style Decisions

- The Lumora mark is always the softened four-point mineral-teal plus; header, footer, appointment utility UI, and micro-labels use the same plus vocabulary.
- Appointment and utility pages retain the same editorial clinical language as the homepage through ivory layering, hairline rules, section metadata, and calm asymmetry.
- Final conversion copy is reassuring and specific to care: warmth comes from clarity and gentleness rather than hype.
